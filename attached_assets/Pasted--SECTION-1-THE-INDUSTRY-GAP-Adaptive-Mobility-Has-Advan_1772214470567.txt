🔷 SECTION 4 — CONTROL INTEGRATION
Flexible Equipment Integration

Adaptive vehicles often include multiple systems — lifts, transfer seats, specialty devices, and accessories.

Adapy integrates seamlessly through:

Harness Kits

Purpose-built integration kits designed for specific equipment models. Multiple harness kits can operate within a single installation.

Wireless Controllers

For locations where traditional mounting is not possible — such as inside transfer seats — Adapy enables secure wireless control integration.

This creates a unified control environment across the entire vehicle.
