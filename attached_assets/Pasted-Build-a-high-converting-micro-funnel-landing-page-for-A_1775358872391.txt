Build a high-converting micro funnel landing page for Adapy’s NEMT product.

PROJECT GOAL:
This page is meant to generate inbound leads from NEMT companies. The goal is to clearly position Adapy as different from traditional fleet management, dispatch, and scheduling software. This page should feel modern, credible, safety-focused, and conversion-driven.

CORE POSITIONING:
Adapy is NOT dispatch software.
Adapy is NOT scheduling software.
Adapy is NOT billing software.
Adapy is the intelligence and monitoring layer inside the vehicle.

Traditional NEMT software manages trips.
Adapy monitors what is happening inside the vehicle.

ADAPY NEMT PRODUCT CAPABILITIES TO FEATURE:
- Carbon monoxide monitoring
- Adaptive equipment usage monitoring
- GPS tracking
- Battery voltage monitoring
- Temperature monitoring
- Real-time vehicle/environment awareness
- Safety monitoring
- Predictive service/maintenance insights
- Visibility into wheelchair-accessible vehicle equipment usage and health

TARGET AUDIENCE:
NEMT operators, fleet owners, wheelchair transport companies, medical transport businesses, compliance-minded NEMT administrators, and transportation companies concerned about passenger safety, risk reduction, and fleet reliability.

PRIMARY MARKETING MESSAGE:
“What you don’t see in your NEMT vehicles is what can cost you the most.”

SECONDARY POSITIONING:
If fleet software tells you where your vehicle is, Adapy tells you what is happening inside it.

DESIGN STYLE:
- Clean, premium, professional
- Modern SaaS look
- Strong trust-building design
- Mobile responsive
- Conversion-focused
- Use subtle motion/hover effects if appropriate
- Use strong section spacing and hierarchy
- Use cards/icons for feature blocks
- Make the page feel like a serious B2B safety/compliance solution
- Avoid clutter
- Use a layout that feels polished and investor/dealer ready
