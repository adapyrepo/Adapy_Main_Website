You are Replit AI. Build the “Adapy CDRS Portal” web app — a clinician-first dashboard for Certified Driver Rehabilitation Specialists (CDRS) and OTs who prescribe/verify adaptive mobility solutions and want objective, defensible outcomes data.

WHY THIS EXISTS (CDRS reality)
CDRS professionals evaluate functional limitations, recommend adaptive driving devices, and support safe vehicle access/operation decisions. The portal must help a CDRS document needs, training progress, and real-world outcomes with objective usage/diagnostics data. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

This portal also must integrate cleanly with mobility dealers and align with industry best-practice documentation and recordkeeping expectations in adaptive mobility work (e.g., systematic documentation, traceability/records retained, assessment considerations, fittings often in presence of DRS/CDRS). :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Adapy product grounding:
Adapy’s dealer platform highlights real-time equipment data, remote diagnostics/troubleshooting, service history logging, and fleet-wide analytics — those capabilities should be repurposed for a clinician portal focused on patient independence and safety outcomes. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

PRIMARY GOAL (product outcomes)
Make a CDRS want Adapy in every patient’s vehicle by delivering:
- objective proof of independence & accessibility outcomes
- time-of-day / usage patterns that match real life
- safety & reliability visibility (faults/maintenance) that reduces risk and improves confidence
- one-click, payer-ready documentation packets (VR/VA/insurance-friendly)
- collaboration with dealers (install/service) without drowning clinicians in dealer workflows

NON-NEGOTIABLE: REPORTS
The portal MUST include at least 10 clinician-facing reports that can be generated per patient and over a caseload, with charts + narrative summary + export to PDF and CSV. Include at minimum these 12 reports:

1) Independence Uptime Report
   - Days with successful access/egress sessions vs missed days
   - Trendline by week/month
   - “Independence score” derived from successful sessions, reduced failures, reduced caregiver assists (clinician-entered)

2) Accessibility & Use Success Report
   - Success/failure rate by equipment type (lift, transfer seat, ramp/door, etc.)
   - Failure reasons (fault codes vs user cancels vs environmental)

3) Time-of-Day Utilization Heatmap
   - Sessions by hour-of-day and day-of-week
   - Identifies real-life participation windows and barriers

4) Community Participation Proxy Report
   - Out-of-home session patterns (use session timestamps + optional location tags if enabled)
   - “Activity days/week” proxy (no precise GPS required for MVP)

5) Training Progress & Competency Report
   - Training plan checklist (skills)
   - Session success over time post-training milestones
   - Clinician can certify “independent with equipment” when thresholds met

6) Safety Risk Signals Report
   - Repeat failures, abnormal stop events, unusual session retries
   - Offline duration and “critical alert” history
   - Clearly labels “clinical attention recommended” flags (not medical diagnosis)

7) Reliability & Maintenance Impact Report
   - Correlate maintenance completion with improved success rates and reduced faults
   - Before/after comparisons around service dates

8) Device & Equipment Prescription Adherence Report
   - Compare prescribed equipment settings (clinician-entered baseline) to actual usage patterns
   - Identifies under-use or over-dependence on certain features

9) Funding / Justification Packet (VR/VA/Insurance)
   - Auto-assembled packet with:
     - patient goals & barriers (clinician narrative)
     - objective usage sessions and success rates
     - safety and reliability evidence
     - service history highlights
   - One-click “Clinician Summary Letter” with editable template

10) Warranty / Service Evidence Report (Clinician-facing)
   - When equipment issues impede independence, generate a timeline of failures and diagnostics to support dealer/manufacturer warranty action

11) Caregiver Burden Reduction Report (Clinician-entered + data-supported)
   - Quick weekly caregiver-assist survey (optional)
   - Correlate with increased successful independent sessions

12) Outcomes Dashboard (Caseload View)
   - Compare outcomes across all patients:
     - independence score
     - success rates
     - usage frequency
     - safety flags
   - Filter by diagnosis category, equipment type, dealer, and funding source

REPORT DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
Every report must include:
- Patient identifiers (minimal PII), equipment identifiers
- Date range selector
- Key KPIs with definitions
- Visualizations (charts/heatmaps)
- A narrative “Clinical Interpretation” section (clinician editable)
- Export: PDF + CSV
- Share controls: “Share with Dealer” + “Share with Funding Agency” (role-gated)

CORE PORTAL MODULES (must implement)
A) Clinician Command Center
- Caseload snapshot tiles:
  - Patients active this week
  - Patients with reduced use
  - High failure rate equipment
  - Overdue maintenance impacting outcomes
  - Offline devices > X days
- Alerts prioritized as “Clinical Attention” vs “Service Needed”

B) Patient Profiles (clinician-first)
- Functional goals (structured fields + narrative)
- Equipment prescription summary (what was recommended + why)
- Connected equipment list + status
- Quick view: independence score, usage trend, last successful session, current risk flags
- Notes and attachments (evals, letters, photos)

C) Objective Data Explorer (sessions + diagnostics simplified)
- Timeline of sessions (filter by equipment + success)
- “Explain this failure” view:
  - show device fault codes, last maintenance, connectivity status
  - recommended next step:
    - “Service ticket to dealer”
    - “Training follow-up”
    - “Re-evaluate equipment fit”

D) Documentation Builder (the “make life easy” feature)
- Letter templates:
  - VR justification summary
  - VA/FSS style justification cover letter
  - Medical necessity support letter (clinician phrasing; user-editable)
- Auto pulls KPIs and charts from the last 30/90 days
- Stores versions and exports signed PDFs (signature capture optional)

E) Dealer Collaboration (without making CDRS chase people)
- Secure “Dealer Share Links” for specific reports/tickets (time-limited)
- Service requests:
  - clinician creates a “Service Request” tied to a patient + equipment
  - includes objective evidence
  - statuses: Sent → Scheduled → Completed → Verified
- Messaging thread per service request (basic)

F) Settings & Governance
- RBAC: CDRS Admin, CDRS Clinician, Assistant, Read-only Student
- Audit log for: report exports, share links, service requests, role changes
- Data minimization controls: hide PII by default, show only when needed

DATA MODEL (minimum tables/collections)
1) Orgs (CDRS practices)
2) Users (role, permissions)
3) Patients (minimal PII: initials or patient_code; optional full name; contact optional)
4) PatientGoals (structured + narrative)
5) Dealers (linked for collaboration)
6) Vehicles (optional VIN; make/model/year)
7) Equipment (type, manufacturer/model/serial, install_date, warranty dates, firmware, last_seen, online/offline)
8) Sessions (start/end, duration, session_type, success/fail, error_code, signal/voltage metrics)
9) Diagnostics (timestamped snapshots, fault codes, readings)
10) ServiceRequests (priority, status, assignment to dealer/tech, evidence attachments)
11) MaintenanceEvents (scheduled/completed; dealer references)
12) Reports (type, parameters, generated_by, file path, share token)
13) Notifications (in-app + email placeholder)
14) AuditLogs (who did what, when)
